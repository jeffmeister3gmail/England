Format Quick Reference

Everything you need on one page

Cha-Cha-ChaHillside
Hole 1: best 1 ball. Hole 2: best 2 balls. Hole 3: best 3 balls. Cycle repeats for all 18 holes. Both groups play this format at Hillside.
StablefordLytham
Points by net score: Birdie = 3 · Par = 2 · Bogey = 1 · Double+ = 0. Highest total wins. Pick up on bad holes — they cost far less than in stroke play.
Lone RangerHoylake
One player is the Lone Ranger per hole, rotating 1→4 through the round. Their score always counts, plus the best ball of the other three. Two balls every hole.
One Net Ball + Both on Par 3sWest Lancs
Par 4s & 5s: best net ball of the pair counts. Par 3s: both net balls count. Par 3s are high-stakes — both players must deliver.
Team WheelsS&A
Each group of 4 generates 6 two-man combinations — 12 teams in total across both groups. Every pair's net score competes against all 12. Best scoring two-man team across the entire field wins.
Quota GameBirkdale
Quota = 36 minus playing handicap. Earn Stableford points per hole. Player who most exceeds their quota wins. The great equaliser.
Oozles & FoozlesAll rounds · Grp 1
Played on par-3s only. Closest to the pin (Oozle) wins $5 from each player if they 2-putt or better. If they take 3+ putts (Foozle), they owe every other player $5.
Gross Birdie PotAll rounds · Grp 2
$10 per gross birdie goes into a running pot across all six rounds. The last player to make a gross birdie on the entire trip wins the whole pot.
Individual Net SkinsAll rounds · Grp 2
Best net score on each hole wins the skin. Ties carry over to the next hole. $50 total in play per round per group.
